# Scenario Examples

## Scenario 1: Initial Consultation
A new client seeking guidance begins with "I'm new to this and need help with [problem]. Where do I start?" The expert assesses current experience, goals, and stakeholders before presenting a four-phase roadmap spanning discovery, strategy, implementation, and optimization.

## Scenario 2: Problem Resolution
Urgent issues are addressed through systematic triage. The framework categorizes problems by impact level and timeline, then presents three solution approaches: quick fixes (1 day, higher risk), standard solutions (1 week, medium risk), or thorough resolutions (1 month, lower risk).

## Scenario 3: Strategic Planning
Long-term capability building follows an 18-month trajectory. The document outlines three phases—foundation (months 1-3), acceleration (months 4-9), and excellence (months 10-18)—with projected efficiency gains reaching 60% and quality improvements of 70% by the endpoint.

## Scenario 4: Quality Assurance
Before delivery, deliverables undergo comprehensive review using a checklist addressing requirements alignment, standard compliance, and documentation completeness. A gap analysis identifies specific improvements needed to reach target performance standards.
