# Common Pitfalls & Anti-Patterns

The content outlines five key design mistakes to avoid:

**High-Severity Issues:**
- Beginning with activities rather than objectives
- Selecting assessments based on convenience instead of alignment
- Treating accessibility as a final addition rather than foundational principle

**Medium-Severity Issues:**
- Working without stakeholder input or testing
- Failing to account for learner cognitive capacity

**Core Principle:**
The document emphasizes starting with learning outcomes and working backward. As the guide states, the correct approach is: "Define what learners should do—then design how they'll demonstrate it" rather than creating engaging activities first and determining learning later.

**Key Takeaway:**
Design decisions should follow this sequence: objectives → assessment → instructional methods, with accessibility and stakeholder collaboration integrated throughout rather than added afterward.
