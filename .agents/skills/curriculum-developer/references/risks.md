# Risk Disclaimer

The document outlines five key curriculum development risks with varying severity levels:

**High-Severity Risks:**
- "Assessments not measuring stated objectives wastes learning time" requires using constructive alignment checklists
- "Too much content without scaffolding causes learner frustration" necessitates cognitive load analysis
- "Excludes learners with disabilities; legal liability" demands Universal Design for Learning (UDL) implementation and assistive technology testing

**Medium-Severity Risks:**
- Stale instructional materials need regular review cycles
- Uniform instructional approaches fail to serve diverse student populations; UDL and alternative pathways address this

**Critical Disclaimer:**
The guidance represents general best practices rather than definitive standards. Users must validate alignment with relevant educational frameworks (state, national, or institutional standards) and confirm accessibility compliance against WCAG 2.1 guidelines and applicable regulations specific to their context.
